Why it gets its own room

This proposal corrects the live atlas drift and mirror problem by using fuller planetary element rates, a better Moon model, and dated-event checks against known transits, conjunctions, closest approaches, and NASA eclipse presets.

  • setJulianDay(jd) lets the Antikythera dial drive the whole scene.
  • True-scale mode keeps radii and distances in the same unit.
  • Visible mode enlarges bodies while preserving their proportions.
  • The check harness compares the proposal, the old atlas, and published events.

Earth to map handoff

  1. Keep Three.js in charge above low orbit: Sun, Earth, Moon, mission paths, shadows.
  2. When the camera nears Earth, raycast the globe to get latitude, longitude, altitude, heading, and pitch.
  3. Fade from the 3D Earth into a MapLibre or Leaflet map centered on that lat/lon.
  4. Pass heading and pitch into the map camera so the motion feels continuous.
  5. Fade back out by converting the map center to the matching globe point.

The bridge is not a new atlas. It is a coordinate handoff: globe vector to map coordinate, map coordinate back to globe vector.
