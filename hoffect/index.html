The Hoffman Effect

Cancer cells in many tumor types show an unusually high dependence on external (dietary) methionine — a phenomenon researched by Prof. Robert M. Hoffman (UCSD / AntiCancer Inc.) and sometimes called "methionine addiction." Restricting dietary methionine has been studied preclinically and in a growing number of published clinical case reports as a way to selectively stress cancer cells, sometimes alongside chemotherapy.

This is an early-stage, actively-researched area — mostly preclinical studies plus individual case reports, not large randomized controlled trials. It is not part of standard oncology treatment guidelines, and no methionine-restricting drug is FDA-approved for cancer treatment. Nothing on this page is medical advice — it exists to make primary sources and calculation tools easy to find. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making dietary changes, especially during cancer treatment.
