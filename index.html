Research & Interactive Tools

Exploring systems, consciousness, and the infrastructure of reality

This site houses research and interactive tools spanning political economy, consciousness studies, agricultural independence, and historical pattern recognition. The work here examines how administrative systems create self-perpetuating problems, how perception shapes reality, and how to build resilience against institutional capture.

What This Site Is About

The projects here represent intersecting threads of inquiry:

  • Necronomy Theory – Systems that profit from perpetual crisis management rather than resolution
  • Consciousness Studies – The nature of observation, perception, and autonomous awareness
  • Agricultural Independence – Building resilience against supply chain capture
  • Rhetorical Pattern Recognition – Identifying linguistic structures that precede violence across conflicts
  • Interactive Visualizations – Tools for exploring historical events, temporal relationships, and data

The common thread: examining the infrastructure beneath visible events. Not just what happens, but the systems that make it happen—and keep it happening.

How-To Guides

Using the Kaleidoscope / Antikythera Widget

  1. Click and drag the crank handle in a circular motion
  2. The date display will update as you scrub through time
  3. Release to lock on a specific date
  4. The celestial background will rotate to show the accurate star positions for that date
  5. Use the reset button to return to present day

Using the Digital Reader App

  1. Paste or type text into the input field
  2. Select voice and speed preferences
  3. Click "Read Aloud" to hear the text
  4. For RSVP mode: click "RSVP" and adjust words-per-minute
  5. Words will flash one at a time in the center of your screen
  6. Use spacebar to pause/resume during playback

Navigating the Necronomy Atlas

  1. The globe will auto-rotate showing event locations as colored pins
  2. Click any pin to zoom to that location
  3. The article panel will slide in from the right
  4. The constellation background rotates to show the historical night sky for that event's date
  5. Click outside the panel or press ESC to close
  6. Hover over pins to see event titles and dates
