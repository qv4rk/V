Open tabs

Hit Capture, then switch to that tab and press its read-aloud/listen button — recording starts automatically the moment sound is detected (or immediately if you unchecked the wait option above). The tab keeps playing normally the whole time; this just loops the audio back to your speakers while also saving it. Only one tab can be captured at a time.
