UNLOCK PREMIUM VOICES FIRST

This app uses your browser's built-in TTS engine. Microsoft Edge has 300+ premium voices — far better than Chrome or Firefox. To activate them:

🖥 Edge Desktop
📱 Edge Mobile
🌐 Other Browser
▸Right-click anywhere on this page (not on a button or link)
▸Select Read aloud from the context menu
▸A reading toolbar appears at the top — click Voice options
▸Change the voice once to force Edge to load its full voice list
▸Close the toolbar, then click LOAD VOICES below
💡 This only needs to be done once per browser session. Voices stay available until you close the tab.
▸Tap the three-dot menu (⋯) at the bottom of Edge
▸Scroll down and tap Read aloud
▸Tap the voice name at the top to open voice selection
▸Change the voice once, then close Read Aloud
▸Tap LOAD VOICES below
💡 If ⋯ is at the top instead, your Edge version may differ — look for Read aloud in the same menu. On iPhone, Edge runs on the same underlying engine as Safari (Apple requires it), so this trick and the Edge-TTS engine below can behave the same as Safari, not like Edge on Windows.
