Ideal simply supported square thin plate: fₙₘ = f₁₁(n²+m²)/2, φₙₘ = sin(nπx)sin(mπy). Audio FFT amplitudes excite modes through a Lorentzian resonance curve. Grain movement follows the gradient of time-averaged squared displacement. This is a physically defined model, not a measurement of a particular metal plate; real plate patterns require its dimensions, material, support, excitation point and measured resonances.
