An Heirloom Cookbook of the Pale of Settlement
The Kiev Heirloom
Recipes of the Jewish Kitchen, 1880–1940
Imperial Edition  ·  Philadelphia Remembrance
✦ ✦ ✦
These recipes belong to the era before the great scarcity — the Pale of Settlement in its last years of fat-heavy, fermented abundance.
Dedicated to the grandmothers of Strawberry Mansion, Philadelphia, who cooked from memory and never wrote it down.
